## Upstream relationship

ShaguTweaks-Extras-ClassicAPI is an independent fork of the ShaguTweaks-extras
tree maintained at:

- https://github.com/paokkerkir/ShaguTweaks-extras

The fork retains upstream attribution and the MIT license notice found in that
source tree.

The fork is maintained independently. Nothing in this repository should be read
as a statement that Shagu, paokkerkir, or another upstream maintainer endorses,
supports, or is responsible for this fork or its releases.

## External dependencies

ShaguTweaks-ClassicAPI and ClassicAPI are required external dependencies.
SuperWoW is optional and may be used through ShaguTweaks-ClassicAPI.

Those projects are not bundled into ShaguTweaks-Extras-ClassicAPI merely
because this addon integrates with their APIs or runtime behavior.

See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `LICENSES/` for dependency and license
records.

releases published by the canonical project. Downstream forks may alter
functionality, packaging, licensing notices, or compatibility behavior and
